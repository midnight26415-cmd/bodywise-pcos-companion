Learn, without the late-night spiral

Useful context, in small pieces.

PCOS care is personal and can change over time. These notes are a starting point for questions—not medical instructions.

01 · CYCLES

A 28-day cycle isn't the only pattern.

Cycle timing can vary. Tracking your actual period starts can give you and your clinician a clearer record than an app's default prediction.

02 · DAILY LIFE

One routine doesn't fit everyone.

Food, movement, sleep and stress support wellbeing in different ways. Small, sustainable choices matter more than a perfect streak.

03 · EXPECTATIONS

There isn't a six-month “cure”.

PCOS is managed over time. Care needs can shift; a product or app cannot promise a particular result or replace ongoing care.

Ask what outcomes are realistic for you.
04 · APPOINTMENTS

Your questions belong in the room.

Bring notes about cycles, skin, hair, energy, mood, medicines and supplements. Tell your clinician what matters most to you.

05 · WEIGHT

Weight is one part of the picture.

PCOS can affect how your body responds to insulin. Sustainable routines and individualized care matter; weight alone cannot explain your health.

06 · MIND

Your emotional wellbeing matters.

Living with symptoms can feel heavy. If low mood or anxiety is affecting daily life, consider talking with a trusted clinician or mental health professional.
